# X waitlist campaign: 5 days, $50 cap

Written for: the founder and anyone reviewing the campaign before it spends.

## Budget and schedule

- $10 a day, 5 days, $50 total cap. Daily and lifetime caps set in the campaign itself, not only here.
- Campaign and line items are created paused and only activated after review.

## Audience

- Geographies: Hong Kong, South Korea, United Kingdom, United States, Singapore.
- Follower look-alikes of DefiLlama, CoinGecko and CoinMarketCap accounts, plus interest targeting for DeFi and crypto research. Confirm each handle is allowed as a target before use.
- Placements: timeline posts only. No video in the first run.

## Creative

- Promoted post A (below). Post B is held back for a second run.
- Destination: `https://dyor.cryptoopsec.com/pricing?utm_source=x&utm_medium=paid_social&utm_campaign=x-waitlist-1&utm_content=post-a`

## Conversion event

- Web event tag on the waitlist join page, firing on a successful join (not on page view), so X optimises for sign-ups.

## Read-out

- Bar: 3% of landing visitors join the waitlist. That is an ambitious bar for cold paid traffic. At $10 a day, a 5-day run may only produce a few hundred clicks, so the rate will be noisy.
- Read both cost per join and join rate at day 5. Decide on scale-up only if joins are cheap and the rate holds across at least two days.
- Campaign tag on every join appears in the waitlist CSV, so results split by post and by country.

## Before activation

1. Check the X ads policy for crypto ads in each target country. Approval may be required.
2. Confirm the web event tag fires on a test join.
3. Review the paused campaign against this brief.
